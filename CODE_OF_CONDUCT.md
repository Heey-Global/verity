# Verity community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a community in which people can contribute without
harassment, intimidation, or discrimination. Be respectful, assume good faith,
disagree with ideas rather than people, and make technical discussions
welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's
private information, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, community
spaces, and public situations where someone represents Verity.

## Enforcement

For an immediate safety issue, use the reporting tools of the platform where it
occurred. For private conduct reports, email `hello@verity.build`. Do not file
a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. Include enough context
for maintainers to understand what happened, but do not send credentials or
unrelated private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily suspend,
or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is inconsistent with this
code. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible. A maintainer
who is the subject of a report must not participate in deciding its outcome.
