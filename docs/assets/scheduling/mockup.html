Mockup · Scheduling requests

Offer times, let the guest pick, book it for everyone

You name the windows in chat. Verity holds them in your calendar — colleagues are invited to the same holds — and sends the guest a PIN link served through Uplink. The guest picks a start time; Verity re-checks the calendars, turns the hold into the appointment and cancels the rest. The calendar stays the source of truth: every page view reads it live.

Decisions shown in this mockup

  • One hold per window, not per slot. Its live position in the calendar defines the offered window, so moving or resizing a hold needs no extra rule.
  • Start times = window ÷ duration, starting at the window's beginning; a slot must fit completely. Each slot is also checked against appointments of every participant whose calendar Verity can read.
  • Holds don't block each other; appointments do. Partial overlap with an appointment removes a slot.
  • Colleagues are invited to the holds. Whether a hold shows as busy for them depends on their calendar; without read access their availability is shown as "Not checked".
  • Booking closes after 7 days (adjustable, shorter); holds are removed at that moment. The Uplink link stays up to day 30 to show the expiry page.
  • Booking requires an explicit button (POST). Link previews and mail scanners that open the URL never book.
  • The guest page shows names, not calendars: no other events or busy times of you or your colleagues are visible.
